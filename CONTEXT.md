Project Context: Kelvin Nimely's Portfolio Website

Overview
Kelvin Nimely's portfolio website is a personal branding platform showcasing his skills, projects, and professional journey as an aspiring software engineer and IT professional. The site will serve as an online resume, a project showcase, and a contact point for potential employers, collaborators, and clients.

Structure & Features
1. Sidebar Navigation (Fixed Panel on the Left)
Displays Kelvin’s profile picture, name, and title.
Provides navigation links to the different sections of the website.
Contains social media links for professional networking (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter).
Includes a certifications section displaying Google IT Support Professional Certificate and Meta Frontend Developer Professional Certificate.
2. Home Section (Landing Page)
A hero section with a headline emphasizing Kelvin’s expertise and career focus.
A brief introduction about Kelvin.
Call-to-action buttons to view projects and download resume.
3. About Section
Detailed background, including education (Starz College of Science and Technology).
Overview of skills and technologies, including JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Django, and AWS.
Career goals and interests.
4. Projects Section
A grid-based layout showcasing personal and professional projects.
Each project includes a title, image, tech stack, description, and links to GitHub or live demos.
5. Certifications Section
Highlights Kelvin’s certifications:
Google IT Support Professional Certificate
Meta Frontend Developer Professional Certificate
Brief descriptions of each certification and their relevance to his skillset.
6. Resume Section
An embedded PDF of Kelvin’s resume.
A "Download Resume" button for easy access.
7. Contact Section
A simple contact form (name, email, message).
Direct contact information including email and LinkedIn profile.
Design & Styling
Minimalist UI with a dark theme and soft shadows.
Glassmorphism-inspired sidebar for a modern aesthetic.
Smooth animations and hover effects to enhance user experience.
Fully responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
Technology Stack
Frontend: Vite.js, React with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN UI
Backend (if needed for contact form or CMS): Node.js, Django, or Firebase
Deployment: Vercel, Netlify, or AWS
Purpose & Goals
Establish Kelvin's online presence as a software engineer.
Showcase projects and technical skills in an organized and professional manner.
Improve networking opportunities with potential employers and collaborators.
Provide an easy way for visitors to contact Kelvin for job or collaboration opportunities.
